Before and alongside my academic work, I worked on applied statistical projects in public-sector and consulting settings.

Ministry of Finance

From 2017 to 2019, I worked as a Data Analyst at the Ministry of Finance, where I conducted statistical analysis of HR and wage data using BI tools, R, SPSS, and SAS.

I later worked as a Statistical Consultant at MAYA Analytics - SAS, where I consulted for the Ministry of Finance and led various data-analysis projects.
